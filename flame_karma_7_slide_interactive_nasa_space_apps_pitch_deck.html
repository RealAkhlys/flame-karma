02 · The Mission Stakes

The Lunar Habitat Paradox: 0.16g + 34% Oxygen

Why future Artemis Moon bases create an unprecedented fire safety challenge—and why Earth intuition fails completely.

01 · EVA DECOMPRESSION CONSTRAINT

Elevated Cabin Oxygen (30–34% O₂)

To prevent astronaut decompression sickness ("the bends") before frequent lunar spacewalks, Artemis habitats operate at lower pressure (~55–70 kPa) with up to 34% O₂—sharply dropping ignition thresholds.

02 · THE PARTIAL-GRAVITY TRAP (SoFIE)

0.16g Feeds Fire Without Cooling It

NASA's SoFIE research reveals that in 1/6g Lunar gravity, weak buoyant plumes bring fresh oxygen to the flame base yet lack the speed to cool it—accelerating flame spread over solid polymers.

06 · From Research to Crew Survival

Citation-Grounded Safety & ISS Flight Rule 7.4.2

Every recommendation links to verifiable NASA PSI / NTRS evidence and generates actionable emergency checklists for flight controllers and astronauts.

STEP 01 · CRITICAL

Isolate Cabin HVAC Airflow

Trip ventilation circuit breakers immediately. Depriving the fire of forced flow (15–25 cm/s) chokes microgravity flames in their own stagnant CO₂ boundary layer.

Source: PSI-98 (Saffire-I)
STEP 02 · ELECTRICAL

De-Energize Rack Power Bus

Cut electrical power to affected EXPRESS avionics racks to halt arc ignition and prevent Kapton/PTFE wire insulation pyrolysis and toxic HCN off-gassing.

Source: PSI-25 (BASS-II)
STEP 03 · SUPPRESSION

Deploy Fine Water-Mist / CO₂

Discharge portable fine water-mist extinguisher into the rack port—cooling cool-flame embers (600–800 K) without scattering burning droplets in zero-g.

Source: PSI-69 (FLEX)
STEP 04 · RECOVERY

ECLSS Scrubbing & Verification

Don emergency O₂ masks, scrub combustion byproducts via closed-loop life support, and verify CO/HCN clearance with Dräger tubes before restoring airflow.
